Remembering 1994

In memory of the victims of the Genocide against the Tutsi

This space is created to remember and honor the lives lost in 1994, and to reflect on unity, humanity, and remembrance.

What Happened

In 1994, Rwanda experienced one of the darkest moments in its history. Over 100 days, many innocent lives were lost.

1994 Timeline

April 1994

The violence began and quickly spread across the country.

May 1994

The killings intensified, affecting many communities.

June 1994

The situation remained severe as efforts to stop it continued.

July 1994

The genocide came to an end and the country began healing.

Reflection

Remembering helps us learn from the past. Never Again.
