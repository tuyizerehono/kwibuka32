Remembering 1994

In memory of the victims of the Genocide against the Tutsi

This website is created in remembrance of the lives lost during the 1994 tragedy. It is a space to reflect, to honor, and to remember the importance of unity and humanity.

What Happened

In 1994, Rwanda experienced one of the darkest moments in its history. Over a period of about 100 days, countless innocent lives were lost in the Genocide against the Tutsi.

This tragedy left a deep impact on the nation and continues to remind the world of the importance of peace, unity, and remembrance.

1994 Timeline

April 1994

The genocide began in April following the plane crash of President Habyarimana. Violence quickly spread across the country.

May 1994

The killings intensified, affecting many communities while the world watched with limited intervention.

June 1994

The situation remained severe as violence continued, and efforts to stop it were still not enough.

July 1994

The genocide came to an end as the country began the long journey toward healing and rebuilding.
