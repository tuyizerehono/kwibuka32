Remembering 1994

In memory of the victims of the Genocide against the Tutsi

This space exists to remember the lives lost, to reflect quietly, and to hold onto the importance of unity and humanity.

What Happened

In 1994, Rwanda went through one of the darkest periods in its history. Over 100 days, countless innocent lives were lost. Remembering this history is part of ensuring it is never forgotten.
